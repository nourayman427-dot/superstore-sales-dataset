# superstore-sales-dataset
This project focuses on analyzing sales transactions to uncover meaningful business insights.
It includes data cleaning, transformation, exploratory data analysis , and visual dashboards that help understand product performance, and revenue trends and consumers behavior.

This project provides a multi-page reporting experience that helps users answer key business questions like:
Which regions and states generate the most revenue?
What product categories drive sales vs. profit?
How do monthly sales trends evolve throughout the year?
